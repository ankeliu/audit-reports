Convert a published report

Give the link to your firm's published report, or its PDF. It is converted to the standard format, the result is posted on a GitHub issue in about two minutes, and a PR is opened for an engineer at your firm to review. Submit only once your firm would publish the report anyway.

Works for Halborn report pages and Runtime Verification and Veridise PDFs. Other firms: check and submit a standard-format file.

Check a standard-format file

Already have a report in the standard format (for example, written from the template, or a converted draft you've edited)? Paste or drop the .md to check it against the same schema and rules CI runs. It stays in your browser. For a PDF or a link, use Convert a published report.
