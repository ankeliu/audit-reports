Submit a published report

Give the link to your firm's published report, or its PDF. The repo converts it to the standard format and replies on a GitHub issue in about two minutes, then opens a PR for an engineer at your firm to review and sign off. Submit only once your firm would publish the report anyway (client approval given, embargoes elapsed).

Converters exist for Halborn report pages and Runtime Verification and Veridise PDFs. Other firms: check and submit a standard-format file.
